# Turan reverse search state-budget sweep

Produced by `water_sort_cli sweep --strategy reverse-search --search-depth 300 --layouts standard,distributed --steps 1000,3000,10000,30000,100000 --samples 300 --max-extra-steps 100 --rollouts 0 --base-seed 0` (release build, 3 threads; default `GenConfig`: `min_opt` 1, `max_attempts` 10000, `max_states` 5e6). Configurations: every supported one (D3) unless `--configs` is given.

`steps` is the search's distinct-state budget (`max_states`). `constr. %` is the share of attempts where no visited state scored above 0 (D16). Walk columns do not apply.

Generation columns are over the generated puzzles: `constr. %` is the share of attempts rejected by the construction before solving (standard layout: the walk did not return; D15), `solved/min %` the share rejected as already solved or below `min_opt`. Walk columns are over the same number of independent scramble candidates: `dead end %` is the share of walks absorbed in a state with no reverse move (a state no pour can lead to), `no return %` the share that did not reach the standard layout, and `extra` the extra steps of those that did (p50 / p99 / max).
