# `uniform` generator statistics

Variant `fisher_yates(layout=distributed)`, layout `distributed`.

Produced by `water_sort_cli stats --generator uniform --layout distributed --colors 2..=12 --capacity 3..=5 --empty 1..=2 --samples 1000 --max-states 5000000 --max-attempts 10000 --min-opt 1 --base-seed 20261002` (release build, 3 threads, batch 64, cell budget 900 s). Sample `i` of every cell uses seed `splitmix64(base_seed ^ i)`.

Rates are per attempt. `solve` is the solver wall time of each attempt (all outcomes); `acc. p99` only over accepted attempts; `states` is states expanded per attempt. Timing columns depend on the machine and on parallel load; all other columns are deterministic. Unsolvable, timeout and below-min rates do not add up to the rejection rate when the construction itself rejects attempts (Turan, D15); those count as attempts without a solver run. `sym` is the fraction of generated puzzles with a nontrivial symmetry (D2); `gen` is the whole generation of one puzzle (all attempts plus metrics). Supported (proposed D3): complete, no failed samples (a sample fails when it hits `max_attempts`), solve p99, acc. p99 and gen p99 < 1000 ms, timeout rate < 0.1 %, and attempts p99 ≤ `max_attempts` / 10. A cell stops early once it has at least 10 timeouts and a timeout rate above 1 % (`stopped:timeouts`), or 10 failed samples (`stopped:failures`); larger `n_colors` with the same capacity and `n_empty` are then skipped. Default solver limit: 5000000 states.
